# Privacy Policy for Herdsman

Last updated: 18 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Herdsman LTD** ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares personal data when you use the **Herdsman** mobile application (the "App").

Herdsman is a workforce and field operations app used to support engineer and maintenance workflows, including timesheets, worksheets, job updates, quotes, purchase orders, chat, vehicle information, and related operational records.

## 1. Who This Policy Applies To

This Policy applies to users of the App, including employees, contractors, engineers, and other authorised personnel using the App on behalf of an organisation.

If you use the App through your employer or another organisation, that organisation may also be a controller of your personal data and may have its own privacy notices or internal policies that apply to your use of the App.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on how the App is used, we may collect and process the following categories of information:

### Account and login information

- Company or tenant name
- Username or alias
- Password
- Authentication token
- Tenant or account identifier

### Device and technical information

- Persistent device UUID generated by the App
- Device operating system and OS version
- App version
- Diagnostic and log information generated during App use

### Operational and job-related information

- Timesheet records
- Check-in and check-out timestamps
- Worksheet data
- Quotes
- Purchase orders
- Vehicle and maintenance-related records
- Engineer profile details returned by the backend, such as name, alias, email, and schedule data

### Location information

- Precise location data at the time of timesheet check-in and check-out, including latitude and longitude

### Photos, attachments, and signatures

- Before and after worksheet photos
- Quote photos
- Purchase order photos
- Images attached in chat
- Tradesman and authorised signatures

### Deep link and setup information

- Information contained in onboarding or engineer bootstrap links used to configure or activate the App

## 3. How We Collect Information

We collect information:

- Directly from you when you log in, complete forms, upload photos, attach files, or submit records
- Automatically from your device when the App records technical details needed for authentication, syncing, support, security, and compatibility
- From your organisation’s backend systems when work records, engineer details, vehicle data, configuration, and related operational data are synced to the App

## 4. How We Use Information

We use personal data to:

- Authenticate users and maintain secure sessions
- Configure the App for the correct organisation or tenant
- Provide App features such as timesheets, worksheets, quotes, purchase orders, chat, and vehicle workflows
- Record check-in and check-out events, including associated location data
- Upload photos, signatures, and other job documentation
- Synchronise data between the App and backend services
- Troubleshoot issues, maintain service reliability, and review diagnostic logs
- Deliver app updates and improvements
- Protect the security, integrity, and authorised use of the App
- Comply with legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations

## 5. Permissions and Device Access

The App may request access to the following device features:

- **Camera**: to capture worksheet, quote, purchase order, and chat images
- **Photo library/files**: to attach existing images or selected files
- **Location**: to record your location when checking in or out of timesheets

If you deny certain permissions, some App features may not function properly.

## 6. Local Storage on Your Device

The App stores information locally on the device to support offline use, session continuity, and operational workflows. This may include:

- Login details
- Authentication tokens
- Synced job and operational records
- Photos and file paths
- Signatures
- Diagnostic log files

The App also stores a persistent device UUID in secure device storage.

Because the App is designed for operational use, some information may remain on the device until it is deleted through the App, removed by your organisation, or deleted when the App is uninstalled.

## 7. Sharing of Information

We may share information:

- With your organisation or tenant that provides or administers your access to the App
- With backend service providers and infrastructure used to operate the App and sync data
- With update and distribution providers used to deliver app updates
- With service providers acting on our instructions for hosting, maintenance, technical support, and security
- Where required by law, regulation, court order, or legal process
- Where necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims
- As part of a business transfer, merger, acquisition, or asset sale

We do not use the App, based on the current implementation reviewed, for third-party advertising SDKs or consumer ad targeting.

## 8. International Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than the country where you use the App, depending on where our servers, your organisation’s systems, or service providers are located. Where required, we will take appropriate steps to protect personal data transferred across borders.

## 9. Retention

We retain personal data for as long as reasonably necessary for:

- Providing the App and related services
- Supporting operational, audit, and record-keeping requirements
- Meeting contractual obligations with your organisation
- Resolving disputes and enforcing agreements
- Complying with applicable law

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of data and your organisation’s policies.

## 10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal data. These measures may include authenticated API access, local secure storage for some identifiers, and access controls.

No method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 11. Your Rights

Depending on your location and the role of your employer or organisation, you may have rights to:

- Request access to personal data
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of personal data
- Request restriction of processing
- Object to certain processing
- Request portability of personal data
- Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent
- Lodge a complaint with a data protection regulator

If your organisation controls your use of the App, you may need to contact your employer or system administrator first.

## 12. Children

The App is intended for business and workforce use and is not directed to children.
